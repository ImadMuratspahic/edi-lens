# Terms of purchase and use

Last updated: 2026-11-12 (EDI Lens 0.2.1). These terms summarise, in plain words, how EDI Lens is offered. The binding text is the End User Licence Agreement in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt); where the two differ, the agreement applies.

## What is free

Every feature that is not marked "Pro" is free: syntax colouring, outline, hover, Pretty Print, Minify, Format Document, Go to Segment, Copy Path, Jump To Matching Trailer, structural validation and the status bar. The free features need no account, no key and no network connection, and they will not be moved behind the licence key in a later version.

## The trial

The Pro features (Anonymise Message and Anonymise Folder, partner rules, Query, the envelope tools (split, merge, renumber, test acknowledgement), Compare With Another File, Export As JSON/CSV/XML, Validate Workspace, Load Dictionary, Batch Pretty Print Folder) can be tried free of charge in a 14-day trial per computer. The trial starts the first time you run a Pro command, not when you install the extension. It is recorded only in the editor's storage on that computer and is not renewed. **EDI Lens: Show Licence Status** shows how many days are left.

## The Pro licence

* **Two kinds, same features.** **Pro Personal**, USD 29 once, is for one person who pays for the licence themselves; use at work is allowed. **Pro Business**, USD 59 per user once, is for companies and other organisations that pay for the people who use Pro. Prices are those at the time of writing; the checkout page shows the final price and the taxes for your country before you pay. There is no subscription and no renewal fee.
* **Scope.** One key is a single-user licence: it is used by one person and may be active on up to three computers of that person at the same time. This is the same for Personal and Business.
* **Business keys.** One key per user: set the number of users with the Buy command, or add ?quantity=N to the checkout link. A Business key belongs to the organisation and may be moved to a colleague (an employee, or a contractor working for the organisation): the previous user runs **EDI Lens: Remove Licence From This Machine** on each computer, then the new user enters the key. One key never covers two people at the same time.
* **Duration.** The key does not expire and covers every update published as EDI Lens.
* **Moving.** Run **EDI Lens: Remove Licence From This Machine** on the old computer, then enter the key on the new one. If the old computer is gone, contact support so the activation can be released.
* **Not allowed.** Publishing, reselling or giving away a key; passing a Personal key to another person; moving a Business key outside the organisation that bought it; using one key for more than one person at a time; bypassing the licence check or the trial.
* **How it is sold.** Only through the online checkout. Purchase orders, invoices with payment terms, custom contracts and vendor or security questionnaires are not offered.

## Payment, invoices and refunds

Licence keys are sold through Dodo Payments, the payment provider and **merchant of record**. The purchase contract for the key is between you and the merchant of record; its buyer terms apply to payment, invoices and refunds, and refund requests are handled by it under those terms. The author of EDI Lens does not process payments and never sees card or bank details. The keys are e-mailed to the address you enter at checkout right after the payment.

## Data

EDI Lens sends none of your files or file names anywhere and has no telemetry. The only network request is the licence check with the payment provider after you enter a key; the trial uses no network at all. Details are in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

The author's liability is limited to the price you paid for the key (zero for the free features and the trial), as far as the law allows; mandatory consumer rights are not affected.
