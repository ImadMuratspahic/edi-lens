**Trial.** The first Pro command you run starts a free 14-day trial of every Pro feature. The trial is recorded only on this computer (in the editor's own storage) and needs no account, key or internet connection. **EDI Lens: Show Licence Status** shows the days left.

**Pro.** A licence key is a one-time purchase and never expires: Pro Personal (USD 29, for one person who pays for it themselves) or Pro Business (USD 59 per user, for organisations), at the time of writing; the checkout page shows the final price and taxes. Each key works on up to three computers of its user. **EDI Lens: Buy Pro** asks which licence you want and opens the checkout page; the key arrives by e-mail and is entered with **EDI Lens: Enter Licence Key**. Only the licence check uses the network, and only after you enter a key.
